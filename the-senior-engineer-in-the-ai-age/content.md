Seniority is not about years of experience. It is about the quality of decisions you make and your ability to help others make better ones.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Core Problem

The best career advice I ever received was to optimize for learning, not for title. Everything else follows.

Technical leadership means making yourself unnecessary. If the team cannot function without you, you have failed as a leader.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What I Learned

The most valuable engineering skill is communication. Code is a means of communicating intent, and so is everything else you do.

The pattern repeats across every project I have worked on. The mechanical
work gets easier and faster, but the decisions that frame it remain
stubbornly human. Tools change. Judgment endures.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Closing Thoughts

There is no shortcut to good engineering. There are better tools, faster
feedback loops, and more powerful abstractions. But the work of understanding
the problem, choosing the right tradeoffs, and building something that lasts
remains fundamentally a human endeavor.

The best advice I can offer is this: invest in your judgment. The rest
can be automated.
