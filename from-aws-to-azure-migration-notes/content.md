Multi-cloud is a strategy for large organizations with specific compliance requirements. For everyone else, it is unnecessary complexity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Core Problem

The best cloud architecture minimizes vendor lock-in at the boundaries, not at every layer. Some lock-in is the price of productivity.

Cloud migration is not a technology project. It is a process and culture change that happens to involve technology.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What I Learned

Cloud computing is not cheaper. It is more flexible. Those are different things, and confusing them leads to bill shock.

The pattern repeats across every project I have worked on. The mechanical
work gets easier and faster, but the decisions that frame it remain
stubbornly human. Tools change. Judgment endures.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Closing Thoughts

There is no shortcut to good engineering. There are better tools, faster
feedback loops, and more powerful abstractions. But the work of understanding
the problem, choosing the right tradeoffs, and building something that lasts
remains fundamentally a human endeavor.

The best advice I can offer is this: invest in your judgment. The rest
can be automated.
