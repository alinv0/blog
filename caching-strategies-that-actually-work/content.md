Performance is a feature. Users do not consciously think about speed, but it shapes every interaction they have with your application.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Core Problem

Caching is the most powerful optimization and the most dangerous. Every cache is a lie waiting to expire.

Premature optimization is the root of all evil, but mature optimization is the root of all performance. Know the difference.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What I Learned

The fastest code is the code that does not run. The fastest network call is the one you do not make.

The pattern repeats across every project I have worked on. The mechanical
work gets easier and faster, but the decisions that frame it remain
stubbornly human. Tools change. Judgment endures.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Closing Thoughts

There is no shortcut to good engineering. There are better tools, faster
feedback loops, and more powerful abstractions. But the work of understanding
the problem, choosing the right tradeoffs, and building something that lasts
remains fundamentally a human endeavor.

The best advice I can offer is this: invest in your judgment. The rest
can be automated.
